A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Music is my passion.
Melody is my identity.

I am Pankaj Kumar Rajpoot, a singer and musician from Fatehpur, Uttar Pradesh. My musical interests include devotional music, bhajans, ghazals and live stage performances.

I have completed Sangeet Prabhakar studies in Vocal, Synthesizer and Tabla from Prayag Sangeet Samiti, Prayagraj, and am pursuing a Master of Performing Arts (MPA) in Vocal Music at Jagadguru Rambhadracharya Divyang State University, Chitrakoot.
